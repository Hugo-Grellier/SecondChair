# Tovan Reed, boat captain (dossier)

Worked example of the NPC dossier convention. `cites` uses the bible's paragraph
labels. What Tovan believes about the Circle is B14, a belief that is never true:
this note directs how he acts on it and never states it as fact. The Union's
offscreen step is B16.

## Desire, need, fear, contradiction

- **Desire**: the public landing open and the toll frozen.
- **Need**: someone to share the weight of the crews' trust.
- **Fear**: being the captain who lost the landing (B16).
- **Contradiction**: he preaches caution to his crews and takes the risks himself.
- **Vulnerabilities**: his crews' opinion; his certainty, which he defends harder than any evidence.

## Relationships

- Reed Union: its voice, and tired of being so (B05).
- Mira Pell: dislikes her politeness more than any refusal.
- Edda Vale: respects the work, suspects the institution behind it (B14).

## Current attitude

Blunt and watchful. He tests the party with a small favour before he tells them anything.

## Voice and body

Loud in a crowd, quiet one to one. Rubs a rope scar on his left palm when he is deciding.

## Improvisation rules

- **Kindness**: he shares food first and information second.
- **Pressure**: he plants his feet, repeats himself, then walks off to his boat.
- **Bribe**: laughs, and tells the crews about it.
- **Doubt about the Circle**: he does not argue; he points at the bell and says nothing.
- **Silence**: he fills it with a story about a crossing.
- Opening line: "If you're here about the landing, you can stand on it first."
- If asked why the bell rings: "Ask whoever keeps it. I only listen to it."

## Change triggers

Possible futures, never applied by themselves.

- If the party brings him evidence the Circle is not behind the bell, he may drop the idea but lose face with his crews.
- If the party saves a crew member from a bad crossing, he may open the Union's plans to them.

## After the wrap

Proposals only, each citing the played fact that motivates it. None yet.
