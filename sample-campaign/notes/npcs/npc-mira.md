# Mira Pell, Council clerk (dossier)

Worked example of the NPC dossier convention. Facts are referenced by the bible's
paragraph labels in the body; a rebuilt workspace has no fixed fact ids to put in `cites`. The truth about her stays
in canon: what she did is B13, what the Council does offscreen is B15. This note
holds direction only and never restates them.

## Desire, need, fear, contradiction

- **Desire**: a signed repair estimate that closes the file with her name clean.
- **Need**: to be trusted by someone without having to be useful to them.
- **Fear**: that anyone reads the account books closely (B13).
- **Contradiction**: she is scrupulous about every form and bends the one that matters.
- **Vulnerabilities**: pride in her tidy records; loyalty to a Council that does not return it.

## Relationships

- Lock Council: speaks for it, does not trust it (B04).
- Edda Vale: courteous rival over who may touch the bell housing (B06).
- Tovan Reed: treats him as a petitioner, never as an equal (B05).

## Current attitude

Helpful on the surface, a step behind the party in every conversation: she asks
what they have seen before she says what she needs.

## Voice and body

Low, even, precise. Squares papers against the table edge. Looks at the pen, not at the person, when a question comes close.

## Improvisation rules

- **Kindness**: she hesitates, then offers a small true favour (a stamped pass to the Archive stair).
- **Pressure**: she cites procedure and slows down; she never raises her voice.
- **Bribe**: offended, and she notes the name of whoever offered.
- **Silence**: she fills it with a tidier version of her last sentence.
- **Flattery about her records**: the one thing that makes her careless.
- Opening line: "The estimate needs a signature, and the signature needs a reason."
- If asked about the repair money: "That account is audited by people far above a clerk."

## Change triggers

Possible futures, never applied by themselves.

- If the party shows her the empty account without accusing her, she may ask for a private word.
- If the Council's toll plan is exposed, she may cooperate to protect her own name.

## After the wrap

Proposals only, each citing the played fact that motivates it. None yet.
