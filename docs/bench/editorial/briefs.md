# The five briefs

Fixed prompts for the public sample campaign, [The Bell Below the Weir](../../../sample-campaign/README.md): Reedbank, the Weir, the Lock Council, the Reed Union, the Sounding Circle and a party of four level-1 PCs. Each producing condition gets the same brief and the same tier. Private or licensed campaigns stay in `secondchair-bench-private` (BEN-06).

| Id | Genre | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| political-intrigue | Political intrigue | Prepare one session where the Council, the Union and the Circle each try to win the party's vote or silence before the festival council meets. |
| dungeon | Dungeon | Prepare a one-session delve under the Weir, with at least three distinct areas, a hazard that changes between visits and more than one way in. |
| character-arc | Emotional character arc | Prepare a session built around Ada Fen: someone from her past reaches Reedbank and forces a choice between old loyalty and the town. |
| mystery | Mystery | Prepare a session investigating who or what is ringing the bell on dry nights, with at least two competing explanations. |
| sandbox | Open sandbox | Prepare an open day in Reedbank and the three outer sites with no fixed plot: eight things that are happening whether or not the party looks. |

Every artifact is written in English, keeps player-facing text apart from GM-only text, and stays within the sample campaign's public material.
