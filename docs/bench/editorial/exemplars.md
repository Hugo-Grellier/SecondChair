# Exemplars

> **Agent-drafted starting point, to be edited by the GM.** Original text. Each shows what a 4-5 looks like on the scale, in miniature. Replace freely; delete this notice per section once the GM has adopted it.

## political-intrigue

The festival council meets at dusk on Bellnight eve. Mira Pell needs a vote to move the toll; she offers the party the repair fee Edda promised, paid from the very fund she emptied. Tovan Reed needs the landing open and will trade a crew's silence about the Council ledger for the party's testimony. Edda Vale needs the bell trusted for one more night and will hide the hairline crack if the party lets her. Each wants something the others can give, fears something the party can find, and pays a price: Mira loses her post, Tovan loses the boat crews' trust if he lies, Edda loses the Circle's independence if she is caught.
Routes: side with a faction, expose the ledger, or force a postponement. Each changes who holds the gate next session. Ignoring the meeting lets the toll pass and starts the Silt Choke clock.
Read-aloud (the hall, dusk): *"Lamp smoke, wet wool, and a clerk's pen scratching one line too many."*

## dungeon

Three areas under the Weir. The Sluice Gallery (flooded knee-deep, slows movement, hides a rusted winch). The Relay Vault (the cracked relay pulses with each bell; a Crafting or Athletics check shuts the pulse for ten minutes). The Silt Cellar (collapsed in the north; a cheap way to bypass the Gallery).
Entries: the bell housing stair, the Cistern conduit, the Kiln drain. Each lands in a different area. The hazard: when the bell rings, the Gallery rises one step, so a second visit meets different terrain.
Encounter notes: one level-appropriate creature per area, never more than the budget; one non-combat resolution each. Treasure: a replacement relay bearing the repair account number, which doubles as a clue. If the party skips the Vault, the bell still fails on Bellnight and the town reacts.

## character-arc

Ada Fen, carpenter. Her old master, Joss Harl, arrives on the ferry asking for her help re-timbering the Weir gate frame, secretly to prove it was built wrong by his rival's hand, which Ada helped cover up years ago. He wants a witness, she fears the town learning she lied, and the cost is her workshop's standing.
Beats: arrival (warm), the frame inspection (a check with a personal edge), the confession (Ada's player chooses what she says first), the council scene. Other PCs have hooks: Beren carried the letter that summoned him; Dain surveyed the frame.
Whatever Ada chooses, Joss and the Council react differently, and the Weir's frame is either repaired, condemned or left to fail. No choice is a wrong one.

## mystery

Two explanations hold: a hairline crack in the relay (true) and Mira's diverted repair fund (also true, but not the cause). Tovan's belief that the Circle rings false warnings is wrong. Three clues point to each conclusion, spread over the Weir, the Archive, the Kiln and the Cistern, so any two sites suffice. Proactive leads: Edda asks the party for help; a boat crew finds the unused relay. If the party misses the relay, the bell fails publicly on the next dry night and hands them the lead. Red herrings are marked as such for the GM and each is disproved by one findable fact.

## sandbox

Eight things happening in Reedbank whether or not the party looks: Sella's satchel went missing at the market; Orin's ferry rope frays; Edda inspects the bell chain; the Council drafts a toll increase; the Union drafts a dredging petition; a stranger asks about the Cistern; the bell rings dry again; the weather turns. Each lists who, where, when it resolves and what changes if nobody intervenes. Three hooks are offered at the market, none is mandatory. A one-page table of who is where by hour closes it.
